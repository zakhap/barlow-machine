Clarence components

The proposed style for everything except the Bloom. Square boxes with 1px lines, columns that share a rule instead of floating, one value box for every number, and segmented switches for every short list. Drag any value box to adjust it, click it to type a number, double-click to reset. Switches and steps click.

2 · Buttons and switches

Buttons drop the letter-spaced caps and go to sentence case, which is most of what reads as "designed by default" now. On is a filled box. Pills only for one-shot actions, like Ableton's Rescan and Browse.

3 · Value box

Every number becomes a label and a fixed-width box, so values line up in a column and you can see what's draggable. Grey text means default, white means changed. Two options: a plain box, or a box with a faint fill showing where the value sits in its range.

B · with fill

4 · Steps

The folded row's step strip as cells. Bar height is the step's weight, a filled bar is a hit, an outline is a flipped step. Beats get a slightly brighter rule.

5 · Rhythm

A rhythm becomes a device: a raised title bar, then columns that share 1px rules, the fan in its own cell, and the strata and tree in a footer row. Fold it with the chevron. The folded row keeps the strip, the three main levers and a list of what's changed. R1 is in Euclid mode. There the meter doesn't pick hits, so the tree gives way to a loudness row: each step's weight as an outline, each hit filled to its velocity, and brackets for the even spacing. Drag Vel depth to flatten or sharpen the accents. Switch Mode, or drag Density and Rotate, to compare.

6 · Instruments and patch

Instruments use the same device shell. In the grid, cards share their borders. The patch is a real grid of cells.

7 · Header

The header as one bar of boxed cells, like Ableton's control bar. Each group gets its own cell and a caption over a value box.
